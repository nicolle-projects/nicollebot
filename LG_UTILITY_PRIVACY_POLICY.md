# LG Utility - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

## Overview
LG Utility is a small Discord bot that **replies with a preset message when a specific keyword is used**.
It stores nothing.

## What LG Utility reads
LG Utility reads messages in the servers it is in **only to spot a specific keyword**.
It ignores direct messages.

## What LG Utility stores
LG Utility does **not store, log, or share** message text, usernames, or user IDs.
It has no database.

## Data sharing
LG Utility does **not sell, share, or publish** any data.
It talks only to Discord and uses no analytics, advertising, or other outside services.

## Data removal
Because nothing about you is stored, there is nothing to remove.

## Children’s privacy
LG Utility is **not intended to collect personal information from children**.

## Changes to this policy

If LG Utility ever starts storing data, this policy must be **updated before deploying the modified system**.

## Contact and Identity Verification

For privacy requests, security concerns, or questions, please contact the bot owner. LG Utility does not provide email-based support to prevent impersonation and maintain Discord-verified communication.
